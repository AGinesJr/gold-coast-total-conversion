# Gold Coast Canon Baseline

**Status:** Consolidated from project conversation. Entries marked TBD require approval.

## Core identity
- Setting: Gold Coast, South Florida-inspired cyberpunk region.
- Period: 2077–2085.
- Protagonist: Rook; lifepaths Corpo, Streetkid, Nomad.
- Central conflict: corporate immortality/control versus human freedom.
- ECHO-9: damaged implant/shard containing reconstructed personality of scientist Elias Voss.
- Elias Voss was killed in 2074.
- Project ECHO: corporate immortality/consciousness program.
- Rook: former Militech intelligence operative; disappeared in 2074; returns in 2077 with damaged implant/ECHO-9.

## Canon change procedure
1. Propose in issue/decision record with rationale, impact, IDs/files and alternatives.
2. Canon Guardian checks contradictions.
3. Human approves/rejects.
4. Update baseline and linked records in PR.
5. Record version and changelog.

Agents may not silently add/rename major factions, locations, protagonist history, timeline events or endings.
